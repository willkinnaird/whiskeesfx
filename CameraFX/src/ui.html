Switching head tracking off here only stops your real head movement. The bumps and lurches keep running. Use this toggle instead of the head-tracking toggles in MSFS or ChasePlane, which switch off the effects as well. If the Tobii loses sight of you, the view eases back to centre and the effects carry on.
